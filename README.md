# 🌿 SehatSense AI
### Smart Food Choices, Better Health.

**A Full-Stack AI-Powered Nutrition & Wellness Platform**  
**Developed by Vikash Mishra**

SehatSense AI is a project I developed to make everyday food choices easier to understand through technology, nutrition calculations, and AI-powered assistance.

The idea behind SehatSense AI is simple: people eat different foods every day, but often don't know how those meals fit into their nutritional goals. I built this platform to help users track their meals, understand estimated nutritional values, monitor hydration and activity, and explore personalized food suggestions in one place.

My goal is to combine modern web development, deterministic nutrition calculations, and generative AI to create a practical wellness assistant that supports healthier everyday habits.

> **My vision:** Make nutrition awareness simpler, smarter, and more accessible for everyone.

---

## ✨ What Makes SehatSense AI Special?

- **Built around real food habits:** Includes familiar Indian foods and portion units such as roti, katori, bowl, and glass.
- **AI + nutrition calculations:** Combines AI assistance with formula-based nutrient estimates.
- **Personalized experience:** Supports food preferences, dietary restrictions, goals, and budgets.
- **Hindi and English support:** Makes nutrition guidance accessible to more users.
- **Wellness-focused design:** Helps users understand their habits without claiming to diagnose medical conditions.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **AI Integration:** Google Gen AI SDK
- **Database:** SQLite / PostgreSQL
- **Authentication:** JWT and password hashing
- **Testing:** Pytest
